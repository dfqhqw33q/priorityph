PRIORITY HANDLING LOGISTICS, INC. — PERFORMANCE EVALUATION SYSTEM
SYSTEM-WIDE UI TEXT & LABELING AUDIT
Date: 27 August 2026
Scope: user-facing labels, headers, card titles, statuses, subtexts, buttons,
table columns, empty states and navigation only.
No database values, enums, routes, permissions or business logic were changed.

=================================================================
0. TERMINOLOGY STANDARDS ADOPTED
=================================================================
The following words are now used consistently everywhere:

  "Self-assessment"        - what the employee fills in (previously "Step 1",
                             "Step 1 assessment", "Step 1 submission")
  "Supervisor review"      - the supervisor's ratings and remarks
  "Step 2 — Conclusions"   - President's conclusions section
  "Step 3 — Reviewing supervisor" - President's reviewing-supervisor section
  "Evaluations to review"  - a person's work list (previously "queue",
                             "review queue", "President queue")
  "Employee records"       - the permanent employee registry (previously also
                             called "Employee profiles" and "Digital Employee File")
  "Activity history"       - user-visible log of actions (previously
                             "Audit timeline", "audit trail")
  "Area"                   - functional part of the system in logs
                             (previously "Module")
  "Record" / "Record type" - the item an action applied to
                             (previously "Entity" / "Entity type")
  "User"                   - the person who performed an action
                             (previously "Actor")
  "Dashboard"              - role home page (no role prefix inside the role's
                             own navigation group)

Step numbers are kept where they appear on the official printed evaluation
form (Steps 1–3), because the paper form uses them. Elsewhere plain language
is used.

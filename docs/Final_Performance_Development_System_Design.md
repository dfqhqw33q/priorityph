# Performance and Development HR Management System

## Final User Roles, Sidebars, Module Mapping, and Workflow

> **Scope decision:** Customer / Customer Integration is completely
> removed from the system. There is no Customer role, Customer Portal,
> Customer Feedback, Customer Rating, Shipment Feedback, or CRM/Customer
> integration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Final System Principle

The system is **one Performance and Development HR Management System**,
not six separate HR applications.

The client's **annual Performance Evaluation** is the core workflow. The
six required school modules are implemented as supporting functions that
use, organize, or extend information generated by the evaluation.

### Six Required Modules

1.  Performance Management
2.  Competency Management
3.  Learning Management
4.  Training Management
5.  Succession Planning
6.  Social Recognition

The modules do **not** need to appear as separate sidebar sections for
every user. Each user sees only the functions required for their role.

# 3. User Roles

## 3.1 HR / Personnel Officer

The main HR/business user.

### Responsibilities

-   Manage employee records
-   Manage 201 files
-   Open annual evaluation cycles
-   Monitor evaluations
-   Complete Personnel Office processing
-   View competency information
-   Manage development records
-   Manage training records
-   Manage career and succession information
-   Manage recognition
-   Generate reports

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.2 Employee / Ratee

The employee participates only in the employee portion of the annual
evaluation.

### Responsibilities

-   Enter or confirm employee information
-   Complete Step 1 A--J self-rating
-   Review information
-   E-sign
-   Submit the evaluation

### Restrictions

The employee cannot access:

-   Step 2 confidential information
-   Step 3
-   Supervisor ratings
-   Personnel processing
-   Committee recommendations
-   President approval
-   HR reports
-   201 files
-   Competency, Learning, Training, Succession, or Recognition
    management screens

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.3 Immediate Supervisor / Rater

The Rater completes the supervisor portion of the evaluation and Step 2.

### Responsibilities

-   View submitted employee evaluation
-   Review employee A--J ratings
-   Enter supervisor A--J ratings
-   Complete Step 2
-   Review AI development/training suggestions
-   Edit or accept AI suggestions
-   E-sign
-   Submit to the Reviewing Supervisor / Division Head

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.4 Reviewing Supervisor / Division Head

The Reviewing Supervisor / Division Head completes Step 3.

### Responsibilities

-   Review the completed evaluation
-   Enter Step 3 comments and recommendations
-   E-sign
-   Submit to Personnel Office

Previous evaluation sections are read-only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.5 Performance Evaluation Committee

The Committee reviews the completed evaluation and makes the final
action recommendation.

### Responsibilities

-   Review evaluation results
-   Review recommendations
-   Select the final action recommendation
-   Recommend retention, transfer, promotion, salary increase, training
    required, or other action
-   Submit the recommendation to the President

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.6 President

The President performs final approval.

### Responsibilities

-   Review the Committee recommendation
-   Approve the final action
-   Return the evaluation for correction/review when necessary
-   Finalize the evaluation after approval

### Restriction

The President does **not** perform:

-   Employee A--J ratings
-   Supervisor A--J ratings
-   Step 2
-   Step 3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.7 System Administrator

Technical and security administrator.

### Responsibilities

-   Manage users
-   Manage roles and permissions
-   Configure system settings
-   Configure evaluation settings
-   View audit logs
-   Manage system activity

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Evaluation Form Structure

The digital evaluation follows the client's form.

## Step 1 -- Employee and Supervisor Ratings

The evaluation contains the A--J performance factors:

1.  Quality of Work
2.  Quantity of Work
3.  Job Knowledge
4.  Ability to Learn
5.  Dependability
6.  Initiative
7.  Human Relations / Teamwork
8.  Cost Consciousness
9.  Discipline
10. Safety Consciousness / Care of Equipment

The employee completes the employee portion of the ratings.

The Immediate Supervisor / Rater then enters the supervisor ratings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 2 -- Rater

The Rater completes the confidential conclusions and comments section.

It includes:

-   Explanation of excellent or poor overall rating, where applicable
-   Principal strengths
-   Principal weaknesses
-   What the employee should do to be more effective on the present job
-   Development potential
-   Advancement outlook
-   Ways to accelerate employee growth and development
-   Interest in another job or transfer
-   Desired job/location, when applicable
-   Qualification, when applicable
-   Other comments or recommendations

**Step 2 is confidential and is not shown to the Ratee.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 3 -- Reviewing Supervisor / Division Head

The Reviewing Supervisor / Division Head completes:

-   Comments
-   Recommendations
-   E-signature
-   Date

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Personnel Office

The Personnel Office section records:

-   Present salary
-   Date of last increase
-   Nature of last increase
-   Amount of last increase
-   Total points
-   Adjective rating
-   Recommended increase / bonus

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Performance Evaluation Committee

The Committee records the final action recommendation:

-   Retain in Present Job
-   Transfer
-   Promote
-   Increase Salary
-   Others (Training Required, etc.)

The Committee Chairman provides the recommendation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## President

The President provides the final approval.

# 8. Gemini 2.5 Flash AI Assistance

Gemini is an assistance layer within the development portion of the
evaluation.

## AI Flow

``` text
Employee + Supervisor A–J Ratings
        ↓
Competency Gap Analysis
        ↓
Gemini 2.5 Flash
        ↓
Development / Training Suggestion
        ↓
Rater Review
        ↓
Use / Edit / Regenerate / Discard
        ↓
Step 2
```

### Example

``` text
AI DEVELOPMENT ASSISTANCE

Employee:
Juan Dela Cruz

Competency Gap:
Job Knowledge

Suggested Development:
Consider job-specific technical training
to improve knowledge of operational procedures.

[Use] [Edit] [Regenerate] [Discard]
```

### AI limitations

Gemini does not:

-   Change official evaluation ratings
-   Approve promotion
-   Approve salary increases
-   Approve bonuses
-   Automatically assign training
-   Make the Committee's final decision

The Rater and management remain responsible for the final decisions.

``` text

        ↓
Learning / Development Record
```

### Example

``` text

Improve Job Knowledge

Supervisor Coaching

```

Possible development activities include:

-   Coaching
-   Mentoring
-   Self-development
-   External learning or training

This is **not an online LMS**.

# 10. Training Management

Training Management records training needs and recommendations.

## Before Committee Decision

An AI recommendation is only a recommendation:

``` text
AI Recommendation

Employee:
Juan Dela Cruz

Suggested Training:
Job-specific technical training

Source:
Gemini 2.5 Flash

Status:
AI Suggested
```

## After Committee Decision

If the Committee selects **Others (Training Required, etc.)**, the
system can create the official training requirement:

``` text
Training Requirement

Employee:
Juan Dela Cruz

Training Needed:
Job-specific technical training

Related Competency:
Job Knowledge

Source:
2026 Performance Evaluation

Decision:
Performance Evaluation Committee

Status:
Required
```

Possible training statuses:

-   Recommended
-   Required
-   Approved
-   Completed

Training may be provided by an external / third-party provider.

The system records the training information; it does not operate the
external training program.

# 12. Social Recognition

Social Recognition is a **system enhancement** because formal
recognition is not a stated section of the client's evaluation form.

It can use finalized evaluation results as one source for identifying
recognition candidates.

## Recognition Flow

``` text
Finalized Evaluations
        ↓
Recognition Candidate Identification
        ↓
HR Review
        ↓
Management Approval
        ↓
Recognition Created
        ↓
Certificate Generation
        ↓
Recognition History
```

## Suggested Recognition Types

### Highest Rated Employee

Based on finalized evaluation results within the same evaluation cycle.

### Most Improved Employee

Based on improvement between finalized evaluation results across
evaluation periods.

### Outstanding Performance

Based on defined criteria and management approval.

### Other Recognition

For management-created recognition such as:

-   Outstanding Contribution
-   Special Commendation
-   Service Recognition

These are system-supported recognition types and should not be presented
as client-defined awards unless the client formally adopts them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Recognition Candidate Rules

Recognition should not be automatically awarded solely from a raw score.

### Example

``` text
2026 Evaluation Cycle

Juan   → 46
Maria  → 45
Pedro  → 46
```

The system displays:

``` text
Highest Rated Candidates

Juan Dela Cruz – 46
Pedro Cruz – 46
```

Because there is a tie, HR / management reviews and approves the
recognition.

Employees should only be compared within the **same evaluation cycle**.

# 19. Final Design Statement

The system is a **role-based Performance and Development HR Management
System** centered on the client's annual Performance Evaluation.

The evaluation serves as the primary source of employee performance and
development information. The A--J factors support competency analysis,
Step 2 supports learning and career/development information, and the
Committee's final action supports official training requirements. Gemini
2.5 Flash assists with competency-gap-based development and training
recommendations, while management retains decision authority.

Social Recognition is implemented as a separate system enhancement using
finalized evaluation results and management approval.

**Customer and Customer Integration are completely outside the final
system scope.**

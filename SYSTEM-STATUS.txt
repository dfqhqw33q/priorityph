4. APPROVED WORKFLOW
======================================================================

1. HR creates an annual evaluation cycle.
2. HR selects the official Performance Evaluation Factors template.
3. HR activates the cycle.
4. The system generates one annual QR/link token.
5. An employee opens the public link.
6. The employee completes and submits Step 1.
7. The assessment becomes available to every authorized Supervisor.
8. No employee, department, division, section, unit, position, or
   assignment filtering is used for Supervisor access.
9. A Supervisor reviews the employee assessment.
10. The Supervisor enters ratings and remarks.
11. The Supervisor submits the assessment to the President.
12. The President opens the evaluation.
13. The President completes Step 2 and Step 3.
14. The system calculates and validates scores.
15. An authorized user finalizes an eligible evaluation.
16. Finalization locks the evaluation and records the final result.

Correction workflow:
- The President can return an evaluation for correction with a required
  reason.
- Existing responses and ratings are preserved.
- The record changes to RETURNED_FOR_CORRECTION.
- The event, previous status, new status, actor, reason, and timestamp
  are recorded.
- Returned records remain visible to the all-Supervisors queue for the
  authorized correction workflow.

7. SCORING
======================================================================

- Scoring rules are versioned.
- Rules are associated with an evaluation template.
- Rules support equal or weighted factor calculation.
- Employee and Supervisor weights are configurable.
- Factor weights are configurable.
- Rating bands define final-rating labels.
- Rounding precision is configurable.
- Calculation status and calculation notes are stored.
- Employee, Supervisor, President, and final score values remain separate.
- Scores are calculated server-side.
- Finalization rechecks eligibility server-side.
- Finalization persists the scoring rule version used.
- Finalized scores are locked.

Finalization checks include the existing server-side eligibility logic,
record version checking, required workflow data, valid scoring rules,
calculated scores, and finalized/locked state protection.
